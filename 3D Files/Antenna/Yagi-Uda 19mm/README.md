A mount for a Yagi-Uda antenna or others that have a 19mm x 19mm x 120mm square tube available.
- 19mm is basically the same as 3/4".
- Designed to directly mount to the skew motor plate (no satellite mounting hardware at all) for either the SM or LG model.
- The base plate is 10mm thick.
- The screw holes are 8mm so the bolts slide through.
- The designed max the head the bolt can have is 11.85mm.
- The mm in the file names denotes the width of the gussets.
- Files without holes are friction fit only. And it is a chef's kiss friction fit.
- Files with holes create a way to add zip ties or other means to help secure the antenna. Unlikely needed, but available.
- If modifying, the gussets will probably give you trouble. I couldn't figure out how to link them to the padding of the base and tube.

I printed the Travler_Yagi-Uda_15mm version with 0.4mm nozzle, 0.2mm height, 5 walls/layers for everything, and 50 gyroid infill in standard PETG. I put the antenna into the print fresh off the printer and it went in with little resistance. After cooling the antenna was not as willing to move. However, I also did not try too hard to move it. In hindsight I would've bolted the mount to the Trav'ler before inserting the antenna for ease of mounting. I used the OEM bolts from the SM model that have a total thread length of 15.5mm. The OEM bolts for the LG only have 7mm of thread before the taper starts so other bolts are needed.
